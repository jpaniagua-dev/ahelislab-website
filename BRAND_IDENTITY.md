# Ahelis Lab — Brand Identity

> Foundational brand document. This file captures the identity currently retained for Ahelis Lab. It should evolve only when brand decisions are explicitly revised.

## 1. Brand

**Official name: Ahelis Lab**

Ahelis Lab is a modern digital studio combining **Strategy · Design · Development · AI** to create digital products and experiences for businesses.

The brand should feel:

- poetic, but never childish;
- technological, but never cold;
- premium, but never pretentious;
- experimental, while remaining credible and business-oriented;
- visually distinctive without relying on generic AI imagery.

Ahelis Lab is not positioned as a conventional “web agency”. The idea of the **Lab** is important: experimentation, prototyping, iteration and the intelligent use of modern tools — including AI and agentic workflows — are part of how the studio works.

## 2. Core message

### Primary tagline

> **The modern way to build your brand.**

### Brand proposition

> **We combine strategy, design, development and AI to build digital experiences that move businesses forward.**

These formulations are currently the reference language for the brand.

## 3. Positioning

Ahelis Lab operates at the intersection of:

- **Strategy** — understanding the business problem before designing the solution;
- **Design** — crafting clear, memorable and useful experiences;
- **Development** — building reliable, maintainable digital products;
- **AI** — using AI as a native production and product capability, not as a marketing gimmick.

The intended perception is a small, highly capable studio able to move from idea to polished digital product with speed, clarity and strong attention to detail.

## 4. Logo and wordmark

### Preferred form

The primary wordmark is:

**AHELIS / LAB**

A variation using a visual separation between the two terms is also acceptable when appropriate:

**AHELIS**  
**LAB**

The word **LAB** must remain visible enough to be perceived as part of the brand, rather than a secondary legal descriptor.

### Alternative exploration

A possible secondary treatment explored for the identity is:

**AHELIS° LAB**

This is an alternative visual route, not the primary reference.

### Usage principles

- Use **Ahelis Lab** in normal written language.
- Use **AHELIS / LAB** as the preferred visual wordmark.
- Preserve generous spacing and a clean composition.
- Avoid decorative complexity around the wordmark.
- Do not distort, stretch or artificially stylise the typography.
- The identity should remain recognisable without relying on the illustrative planet element.

## 5. Visual world

The visual direction is **poetic and cosmic**.

It draws on the emotional qualities of small planets, orbit, exploration, curiosity and imagination, with a refined editorial execution rather than a childish or literal space theme.

A visual reference is the poetic simplicity associated with *The Little Prince*: a small world, a sense of wonder and exploration, translated into a contemporary digital identity rather than reproduced literally.

### Signature visual element

A recurring visual element is a **yellow 3D planet / sphere with an orbital system**.

It can function as:

- a hero object;
- a spatial anchor in compositions;
- a recognisable brand asset;
- a metaphor for ideas, systems, exploration and things being built around a core.

The object should feel crafted and premium, not like a generic stock 3D illustration.

### Visual principles

- Large areas of negative space.
- Strong editorial hierarchy.
- Dark, immersive backgrounds balanced by warm light areas.
- Selective use of bright yellow and sky blue.
- Clean grids and precise alignment.
- 3D used as a signature element, not as visual noise.
- Motion, when introduced, should feel orbital, smooth and intentional.

### Avoid

- generic AI gradients;
- robot, brain or circuit-board clichés;
- excessive neon effects;
- childish astronomy illustrations;
- overly corporate SaaS aesthetics;
- dense layouts;
- decorative effects without purpose.

## 6. Colour palette

The retained palette is:

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary dark | **Midnight Blue** | `#08162B` |
| Secondary dark | **Deep Blue** | `#0D203D` |
| Primary accent | **Solar Yellow** | `#FFD447` |
| Secondary accent | **Sky Blue** | `#7EC8FF` |
| Light surface | **Ivory** | `#F8F6EF` |
| Dark text / detail | **Ink** | `#11131A` |
| Muted UI / text | **Muted Blue Grey** | `#A7B4C6` |

### Colour behaviour

**Midnight Blue** and **Deep Blue** establish the core digital/cosmic atmosphere.

**Solar Yellow** is the main signature accent and should be used deliberately. It is especially associated with the planet / orbital visual.

**Sky Blue** introduces openness and a lighter technological note.

**Ivory** should be preferred over pure white when a warmer, more editorial surface is appropriate.

The palette should maintain strong contrast and remain accessible in interface usage.

## 7. Typography

No definitive type family has been formally locked yet.

The typographic system should support the following characteristics:

- contemporary;
- editorial;
- highly legible;
- premium;
- precise without feeling sterile;
- strong enough for oversized display typography;
- suitable for digital interfaces.

Until a typeface is formally selected, typography should be treated as an open brand decision rather than documented as final.

## 8. Tone of voice

Ahelis Lab should communicate with confidence and clarity.

### The tone is

- direct;
- intelligent;
- concise;
- curious;
- contemporary;
- human;
- ambitious without exaggeration.

### It avoids

- empty agency language;
- excessive technical jargon;
- “AI will change everything” claims;
- inflated promises;
- overly formal corporate language;
- trying too hard to sound futuristic.

The work and the ideas should provide the sophistication; the writing itself should remain clear.

## 9. Brand idea

Ahelis Lab is a place where ideas are explored, shaped and turned into useful digital products.

The **Lab** is not merely a suffix. It represents the working philosophy:

**explore → understand → design → build → test → refine**

AI belongs naturally inside that process, alongside strategy, design and engineering.

## 10. Website direction

The website should make the brand feel immediately distinct from a conventional local web agency.

Priority characteristics:

- strong hero typography;
- a dark/cosmic opening atmosphere;
- the yellow orbital planet as a recognisable visual asset;
- restrained but high-quality motion;
- clear articulation of **Strategy · Design · Development · AI**;
- case-study and outcome-driven communication as the portfolio develops;
- premium execution with very little visual clutter.

The visual identity should communicate experimentation and imagination, while the content communicates commercial seriousness and technical capability.
